      Sync HealthKit data via Shortcuts
      Run the Siri Shortcuts below on your iPhone or Apple Watch to automatically sync sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, and workout data from HealthKit into Peak Tracker.
      Available shortcuts:
      🌙 Sync Sleep ❤️ Sync HRV/RHR 💪 Sync Workout 📱 Sync All
      Last sync Never
      Setup Instructions
      1. Open the Shortcuts app on your iPhone
      2. Create a new shortcut called "Peak Sync Sleep"
      3. Add action: Find Health Samples → Sleep Analysis → Last 1 day
      4. Add action: Get Hours From Date (from sleep duration)
      5. Add action: URL → https://YOUR-VERCEL-URL/peak.html#peak-sync-{"sleep":HOURS}
      6. Add action: Open URLs
      7. Repeat for HRV, RHR, and Workout shortcuts
      8. Add to Apple Watch: tap ⋯ → Add to Apple Watch
